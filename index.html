Empowering Communities in Binga

Inspiring Young Minds, Building Self-Reliance

At Twaabane Primary School, we cultivate faith, intellectual curiosity, and gender equality in an uplifting Christian environment in Binga Centre, Zimbabwe.

Our Mission

Education For Transformation & Equality

To facilitate the provision of education that enhances empowerment of the communities to think in other terms and embraces diversity for gender equality and social inclusion.

Guided by Christian values of faith, love, and prophetic voice, our learners grow into self-reliant, God-fearing citizens.

Active School Community

Safe playground, sports & cultural enrichment

Christian Values & Love

Faith, love, and a prophetic voice driving moral foundation and character excellence.

Gender Equality & Inclusion

Fostering an inclusive space where every boy and girl has equal voice and opportunity.

Self-Reliance & Skills

Empowering learners to think critically, innovate, and contribute to community growth.

Academic Foundation

Our Curriculum

Combining national primary standards with cultural grounding, sciences, and creative arts.

English Language

Comprehensive literacy, grammar, conversational confidence, and creative writing.

Mathematics

Logical thinking, arithmetic speed, problem-solving, and practical numbers in daily life.

Science & Technology

Experiential scientific inquiry, environmental studies, and computing literacy.

ChiTonga / IsiNdebele

Celebrating indigenous languages, Zambezi cultural values, and linguistic fluency.

Social Science

Civic education, national heritage, community leadership, and global awareness.

PE and Arts

Athletic discipline, sportsmanship, traditional dance, painting, and music.

Vibrant Campus Experience

School Life & Activities

Beyond textbooks: a dynamic environment fostering sportsmanship, culture, and team spirit.

Outdoor Sports

Recreation & Athletics

Structured physical education sessions and spirited break-time recreation on our school fields that keep learners healthy, active, and socially engaged.

Early Literacy

Reading Circles & Phonics

Interactive storytelling and guided reading groups that instill a lifelong love of literature from a child's earliest years in primary education.

Hands-on Science

Hands-On Science & Discovery

Practical laboratory experiments, nature walks, and sensory observations that encourage students to hypothesize, test, and understand the natural world.

Collaborative Learning

Group Projects & Teamwork

Collaborative workshops where learners share ideas, respect diverse viewpoints, and create joint class presentations that build public speaking confidence.

Cultural Arts & Values

Cultural Arts & Christian Fellowship

Weekly chapel devotionals, choral music, and cultural arts clubs celebrating Tonga customs alongside Christian values of humility and compassion.

Senior Mastery

Senior Academic Preparation

Intensive revision clinics, mock assessments, and personalized mentorship empowering upper primary learners to achieve top grade marks.
