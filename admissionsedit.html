Registration Requirements

To ensure a smooth enrollment process, please prepare the following documents:

Certified Copy of Birth Certificate
Latest School Progress Report
Two Passport Sized Photographs
Transfer Letter (if transferring schools)
Immunization & Child Health Record
Proof of Residence in Binga District
Simple Steps

How To Apply

A transparent, stress-free admissions workflow for parents and guardians.

1

Fill Form

Complete our online enrollment application or collect a physical form from the front office in Silyambembe Suburbs.

2

Upload Docs

Attach the student's certified birth certificate and relevant health cards using our secure portal upload.

3

Friendly Assessment

Learners participate in a warm, welcoming placement evaluation to ascertain foundational literacy and numeracy.

4

Enrolment & Welcome

Receive your official acceptance letter, settle school fees, and welcome your child into the Twaabane family!
