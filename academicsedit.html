Our Academic Program

Fostering academic excellence, moral discernment, and practical self-reliance from Early Childhood Development (ECD) through Grade 7.

Rigorous Standards

Curriculum & Core Learning

Combining the Zimbabwe national primary syllabus with indigenous languages and digital technology.

Language & Literacy

Equipping learners with strong phonetics, comprehension, speech articulation, and bilingual fluency in English, ChiTonga, and IsiNdebele.

  • English Grammar & Composition
  • ChiTonga Mother Tongue Literacy
  • IsiNdebele Language & Culture

Mathematics & Logic

Developing mental arithmetic speed, conceptual logic, geometry, and practical numbers used in daily problem-solving.

  • Foundational Numeracy & Sets
  • Applied Mathematical Problem Solving
  • Measurements, Graphs & Geometry

Science & Technology

Hands-on observation, nature studies, digital awareness, and practical STEM investigations that nurture curious minds.

  • Environmental Studies & Ecology
  • Physical Sciences & Energy
  • Introductory Computer Literacy
