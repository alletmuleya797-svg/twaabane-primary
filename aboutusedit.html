Rooted in Faith & Community

About Twaabane Primary

Nurturing excellence, fostering self-reliance, and inspiring community leadership in Binga Centre, Zimbabwe.

Guiding Philosophy

Our Identity & Pillars

Built upon Christian values, cultural respect, and equal empowerment for every learner.

Our Mission

To facilitate the provision of education that enhances empowerment of the communities to think in other terms and embraces diversity for gender equality and social inclusion.

Twaabane Primary School's priority is to produce individuals who are self-reliant and guided by the Christian values of faith, love, and prophetic voice.

Our Vision

To establish and administer a leading centre of educational excellence in Binga and beyond through promotion of innovation, real-life competencies, and a holistic personal growth curriculum.

Core Values

  • Integrity: High moral character, truthfulness, and honesty.
  • Service: Dedicated to delivering beyond expectations for our learners.
  • Excellence: Recognizing that every child has the capacity to achieve.
  • Innovation: Embracing technology, STEM, and creative problem solving.
  • Teamwork: Strong partnership between teachers, parents, and community.

Achievements

Celebrated regional champions in inter-school athletics 2025, outstanding pass marks in primary curriculum assessments, and active recognition in indigenous Zambezi culture and arts festivals.

Community Partnerships

Deep collaboration with Binga elders, faith communities, and local councils to conduct tree planting, civic clean-ups, and ensure every child has access to quality education.

Gender Equality & Inclusion

Ensuring both young girls and boys receive equal mentorship, leadership responsibilities, academic resources, and career aspirations from their foundational years.
